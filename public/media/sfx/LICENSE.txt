Sounds in sprite.mp3
====================

sprite.mp3 joins every sound the site plays into one file. sprite.json lists
each clip's position and where it came from (the third field of each entry).
Two kinds of clip are in it.

1. Recordings from Kenney's CC0 packs (6 clips: the clicks)

   Their source file names are in sprite.json: rollover2.ogg, tick_001.ogg,
   click1.ogg, click3.ogg, select_001.ogg, select_007.ogg.

     Interface Sounds  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
     UI Audio          https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio

   by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl). Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0),
   http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   You may use these assets in personal and commercial projects. Credit
   (Kenney or www.kenney.nl) is appreciated but not required.

2. Clips synthesised for this project (16 clips)

   The globe's spin ticks, the extra hover and tap clicks, and the four reveal
   jingles (frost, amethyst, jade, gold). They are generated from nothing but
   sine, noise and envelope maths by scripts/sound/synth.py (seeded, so a
   rebuild gives the same file), and belong to the project. No recording is
   sampled.

Every clip is gain-matched to the loudness plan in app/audio/sound-plan.json
and true-peak limited, then encoded to MP3, by scripts/sound/build_sprite.py.
Nothing is changed otherwise.

The audition page (development only, /sound-lab) also carries held-back clips
and the jingle candidates that were not chosen, in app/sound-lab/assets/. They
are not part of the site and are not shipped. The candidate jingles are
synthesised like the clips above; the held-back clips are from the Kenney packs.
